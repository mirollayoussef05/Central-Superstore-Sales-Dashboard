# 📊 Central Superstore Sales Performance Analysis (Power BI)

An interactive, executive-ready Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance, profitability, and operational efficiency for a retail business.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes retail sales data, tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) like revenue, profits, product segments, and shipping timelines. It is designed to help business managers quickly identify high-profit product lines, seasonal sales trends, and logistics efficiency.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights & Key Metrics

* **Total Sales:** $501.24K
* **Total Profit:** $39.71K
* **Profit Margin:** 7.92%
* **Total Orders:** 1,175 Orders
* **Average Shipping Days:** 4.06 Days

---

## 🔍 Key Visuals & Sections Inside the Dashboard

### 1. Sales & Profit Trends
* **Sales & Profit by Month:** A monthly timeline line chart showing the trend of sales against profitability over the year, helping identify high-performing months.

### 2. Category Performance
* **Profit by Category:** A column chart displaying profit margins across different product lines to pinpoint which items drive the most revenue.

### 3. Product Analysis
* **Top 10 Products by Sales:** Displays the highest revenue-generating products (e.g., Canon ImageCLASS copier) to analyze product popularity.

### 4. Market Segmentation
* **Sales by Segment:** A bar chart segmenting customers into **Consumer**, **Corporate**, and **Home Office** categories to target marketing efforts effectively.

## 💡 Recommended Business Actions based on Dashboard
1. **Boost Low-Margin Months:** Implement targeted marketing campaigns during historically slower months to stabilize sales volume.
2. **Optimize High-Profit Categories:** Allocate more inventory and marketing budget to the top-performing categories shown in the profit breakdown.
3. **Logistics Control:** Maintain the healthy average shipping time of ~4 days, but investigate any outliers causing shipping delays.
